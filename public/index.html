How this dashboard works
Live spend numbers are pulled directly from Meta Ads every minutes — no manual updates needed. Each ad campaign is automatically sorted into the right county based on its name. To change a county's monthly budget, click Edit budget — the new number is saved permanently and used for all future calculations. Hover any metric (the small ? icon) to see exactly what it means.

Hooks

Hook rate = viewers who watched the first 25% ÷ impressions (Meta's API no longer returns 3-second plays). Hold rate = ThruPlay (15s or full) ÷ those hook views. Benchmark: ≥30% good 25–30% near <25% poor — thresholds live in config/accounts.json.
